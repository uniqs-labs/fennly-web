A positive IgG food test mostly shows what you eat regularly, not what you can't tolerate.

There is no single test for every food intolerance, but breath tests, coeliac blood tests and allergy tests answer specific questions when done right. For many other suspicions, cutting out one food for a limited time and then reintroducing it, ideally with a GP or dietitian, is the practical next step.
