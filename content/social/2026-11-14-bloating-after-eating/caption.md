Bloated after dinner? It may not be dinner.

Gas comes from swallowed air and from gut bacteria breaking down undigested carbohydrates, and large meals, eating quickly and fizzy drinks can make it worse. Food intolerance symptoms usually start a few hours after eating, and it often helps to find your own triggers rather than cutting out long lists of foods without advice.
