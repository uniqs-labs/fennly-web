Not absorbing large amounts of fructose is normal, even when healthy. It usually only matters when it causes symptoms.

Fructose malabsorption can cause gas, bloating, tummy pain or diarrhoea, and a breath test with symptom tracking can help a doctor check for it. What usually follows is not a lifelong ban on fruit, but a short phase of eating less fructose and then reintroducing foods one at a time.
