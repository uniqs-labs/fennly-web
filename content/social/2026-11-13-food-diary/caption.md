Blaming your last meal? Symptoms can start hours later.

Food intolerance symptoms usually start a few hours after eating and can last for hours or days, so on a bad day look back 24 to 48 hours. Log amounts, stress and sleep too, and treat what you find as a lead to check, not a diagnosis.
