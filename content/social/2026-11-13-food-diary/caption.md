4 food diary mistakes that hide your triggers.

Food intolerance symptoms usually start a few hours after eating and can last for hours or days, so on a bad day look back 24 to 48 hours. Log what and how much you ate straight after eating, add stress and sleep, and treat what you find as a lead to check with a doctor or dietitian, not a diagnosis.
