Low FODMAP isn't the first step for IBS. Guidelines start with simpler changes.

Guidelines suggest starting with regular meals, less caffeine, alcohol, fizzy drinks and fatty food, then adjusting for your main symptom. Low FODMAP is a time-limited second step with a dietitian, and a coeliac test should come before going gluten-free.
