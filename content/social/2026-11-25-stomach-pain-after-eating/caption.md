Stomach pain after meals? 4 patterns in where, when and how it hurts that can point to the cause.

Burning in the upper tummy soon after eating can point towards indigestion, while tummy pain a few hours later can point towards a food intolerance. Sudden severe pain, vomiting blood or black stools need emergency help straight away.
